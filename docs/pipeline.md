# Cultural compilation pipeline

How a photo becomes discoveries. The behavior rules live in the [build spec](../specs/cultural-discovery-build-spec.md) ("Cultural compilation pipeline", "Qloo integration contract", "Recommendation and memory rules"); this page maps them to code.

## Intake (`packages/domain/src/services/captures.ts`, `media/image.ts`)

1. `normalizeImage` sniffs magic bytes (the declared type and filename are ignored), rejects payloads over `UPLOAD_MAX_BYTES` (413) and images over `UPLOAD_MAX_PIXELS` (checked from the header before decoding), applies EXIF orientation, strips all metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP, ICC) and writes a JPEG derivative bounded to `IMAGE_MAX_EDGE` (2048 px). The sha256 is of the original bytes. JPEG, PNG and WebP are accepted. HEIC is attempted and otherwise gets conversion guidance; AVIF is rejected.
2. `createCapture` accepts multipart bytes, an owned `upload_id` (agents and the sensor bridge), or a `sample_id` ("Try an example": `samples/<id>.<ext>` in storage or `apps/web/public/samples/<id>.<ext>`). It computes `analysisDedupeKey(owner, sha256, focus, COMPILER_VERSION)`, so a duplicate submission returns the existing capture and job with `reused: true`; other owners never share. It consumes the daily analysis quota and creates the capture, its `kind=capture` DiscoveryItem and the `analyze_capture` job (budget `{qloo_requests, research_refs, provider_retries, deadline_ms}`) in one transaction. Sensor imports must come from the bridge token for that connection, are deduped by `(connection, asset_id)` in `sensor_imports`, and are verified against `declared_sha256`.
3. Private storage layout: `owners/<a|s>-<owner>/uploads/<upload>/derivative.jpg` and `owners/<a|s>-<owner>/captures/<capture>/{derivative.jpg,original}`. Originals are kept only with `STORE_ORIGINALS=true`.

## Compiler (`packages/domain/src/compiler/analyze.ts`)

Each stage runs through `ctx.runStage`, so a retried attempt (lost lease, crash, deadline) reads completed outputs back from `job_stages` instead of calling providers again. Publishing is idempotent and always goes through `publishForCapture`: `ctx.ensureActive()` and then a transaction holding the capture row lock (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` where `deleted_at IS NULL`). `deleteCapture` takes the same lock, so a delete either wins and nothing is published, or runs after and removes what was published.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| observe | `observing` | VLM structured analysis of the derivative (focus, confirmed interests and rejected names passed as data). Title, interpretation, observations, visible text, candidates with evidence boxes, and a "What caught your eye?" question when there are 2-3 competing subjects. The first analysis becomes active immediately (progressive results). Corrections activate at the end. Unusable photos get `unclear_photo` guidance and stop. | `observations` |
| guard | (within `observing`) | The reading guardrails (`packages/providers/src/vlm/guardrails.ts`, [quality plan §4](quality-plan.md), `guard-v2`) run on the reading before anything is published: medium words ("painted" counts only outside crafts, interiors, architecture, fashion and vehicles), roles and identities of people, generic objects (demoted to observations), uncertain places and cuisines without food are dropped or demoted; brands and low-certainty claims are **held** for stage 2 (dropped when no stage 2 is planned); visible text keeps transcriptions only (no descriptions; no plate-shaped strings on a reading that shows a vehicle). Only the publish set is published, resolved and anchored. Every decision is in the `guard` trace event (stage 1, and stage 2 after enrich). A reviewed example replay is published as reviewed. | `observations` |
| resolve | `resolving` | Up to 5 strongest candidates (amendment 2.2), at most 3 Qloo requests in flight. The grounding rules (`compiler/grounding.ts`, [quality plan §5](quality-plan.md#5-qloo-acceptance-rules-p3-exact), summarized in [qloo-probes.md](qloo-probes.md#grounding-rules)) decide the lookup: objects are never searched, brands only when their name was read in the photo, places and cuisines only with evidence; creators, works, brands and places go to `/search` with their entity types, styles, traditions and cuisines to `/v2/tags` with tag types. A result counts as the same thing only on a compatible type and an exact canonical name (a brand may add a corporate suffix; a shorter name never matches); several same-named results are a doubt. Each decision is recorded in `query_context.accept`. Matches become public `cultural_references` (`qloo:<id>` / `qloo_tag:<id>`). Unmatched, doubtful and unsearched candidates are kept as qualified references under their own name (`<type>:<slug>`), never under a Qloo look-alike's name, and never anchor. At most 5 anchors are chosen: user selections, then depicted, then certainty. A candidate that resolves to a reference the user rejected stays `rejected`. | `references` |
| recommend + compose | `recommending` | One `/v2/insights` call per category (wear = fashion brands via `filter.tags=urn:tag:genre:brand:fashion`, play = artists, watch = films), with the anchors' real entity and tag ids and explainability on, in priority order from the anchors' suggested categories. Results are filtered (anchors themselves, hidden items, keys already used), fused with RRF (style directions join Wear), diversified by contributing reference, and capped at 3 per category. Ranks interleave categories. Every item is a per-owner `DiscoveryItem` (stable `canonical_key`) plus a `Recommendation` citing its `provider_query_ids` and `contributing_reference_ids`, with a plain-language reason from explainability. | `wear`, `play`, `watch` |
| enrich | `enriching` | Stage 2 of the two-stage photo reading (amendment 2.2, see below). Skipped unless stage 1 was a Concentrate quick read and `fast_analysis_two_stage` holds. Strictly additive: never reorders or removes a published card. | `wear`, `play`, `watch` (append only) |
| research | `researching` | Up to 5 references, anchors first (references added by stage 2 included): Wikipedia sources, then claim extraction. Results go to the public library (`sources`, `claims`, overview, sections). Pages researched in the last 30 days are reused. | `research` |
| eat | `composing` | Dish ideas from supported cuisine, place or tradition references (resolved or researched, never a doubtful grounding) via the composer model. Each must cite a supplied reference. Reasons are shown without dietary safety sentences (`stripSafetyClaims`, as in plans). `origin = 'vlm_creative'`. | `eat` |
| style | (parallel, from t0) | The style reading, its Qloo mapping and the "More like this" rails ([style reading](#style-reading-and-more-like-this) below). Never blocks the stage-1 chips; a failure fails only this section. | `style`, `more_like_this:s1`, `more_like_this:s2` |
| publish | `publishing` | Warnings, active analysis, capture status `ready` / `partial`. | |

Job outcome is `partial` whenever a section is `failed`, `unavailable` or `partial`. When Qloo is unavailable, references are still published, Wear, Play and Watch are `unavailable`, and the analysis carries `discoveries_delayed` ("Related discoveries are taking longer."). Research failures omit historical text.

### Two-stage photo reading (amendment 2.2)

Specs: [`specs/onboarding-and-fast-analysis.md`](../specs/onboarding-and-fast-analysis.md) (FAST-01..03) and the measured schemas in [`docs/analysis-latency-plan.md`](analysis-latency-plan.md).

- **Stage 1 (observe).** A `vision_photo` profile whose `schemaVersion` is `atlas_quick_read_v1` makes `ConcentrateVlm` send the slim `VlmQuickRead` (title, one-sentence interpretation, at most 5 candidates, quality) with the quick prompt, the image bounded to 512 px, and the profile's `reasoningEffort`. `toVlmAnalysis(quick)` turns it into today's `VlmAnalysis` with synthetic ids (`c1..cN`), so everything downstream is unchanged and the first sections publish as before. A profile pinned to `atlas_observations_v2` keeps the legacy single full reading.
- **Plan and budget.** Before stage 1 the compiler decides whether stage 2 will run: a usable stage-2 enricher (a qualified Concentrate `vision_photo_enrich` role; Elorian never serves it), no `fast_analysis_two_stage` or `concentrate_analysis_enabled` kill for the account (env `FEATURE_KILL_SWITCHES` or `feature_flag_kills`), the spend ledger able to reserve both profiles' worst cases together for this job (`ModelSpendLedger.canReserveAll`; both calls share the job id, so they count as one daily analysis), and the job budget's two photo-reading calls (`JobBudget.reserveVisionCalls`). Otherwise the reading is the single stage-1 call and the trace says why.
- **Stage 2 (enrich, after recommend).** One `VlmEnrichment` call on the capture image (≤ 768 px; for v2 media the best frame, see below) with the guarded stage-1 title, the published candidate names and then the held claims as DATA (`stage2Request`; the qualification harness and `eval:live` send the same input), 20 s timeout capped by the soft deadline. The model output is cached by `runStage('enrich')`, so a retried attempt never dispatches it twice; a failure or timeout is recorded (`provider_queries` `vlm`/`enrich_image`, status `error`) and leaves stage 1 exactly as published.
- **Merge (deterministic, `mergeEnrichment`).** Stage-1 candidates keep order, ids and names; confirmations fill rationales; `keep=false` is advisory: certainty × 0.5, the reference stays an anchor and keeps its cards, and `capture_references.advisory_veto` records the decision (`db/migrations/130_fast_analysis_enrichment.sql`). The reference stays in "Explore the references" in the place it was first shown (`published_certainty`), now qualified because its certainty is lower; references stage 2 adds are listed after every stage-1 reference (`enrichment_added`, `160_enrichment_reference_order.sql`). Cards stage 2 appends rank after every card already published, so the first rendered cards never move. Regions, observations, visible text and (when there were none) competing subjects are added; title and interpretation never gain anything; the one exception is a retraction (guard-v10, `headlineChanged`): when what stage 2 shows makes the published headline a claim about the people (a role, relationship, occasion, team or unit, or a withheld people claim), it is replaced by the neutral "People in your photo" line, in the analysis and on the capture card (docs/quality-plan.md §18.20). A "What caught your eye?" question is only added, never replaced.
- **Held claims and corroboration.** A held claim publishes only on a binding stage-2 verdict, through the append path below (`guardEnrichment`); when stage 2 fails or is skipped, held claims are dropped (fail closed). A brand-like claim also needs every one of its brand words transcribed at stage 2 (G-BRAND-CORROBORATE, guard-v3); only those candidates are searched in Qloo as brands (`brandCorroboratedIds` sets `corroborated` for the grounding). A person or creator named at stage 1 is held the same way until stage 2 transcribes the name. Additions pass the same rules, and a brand or person named only in the stage-2 reply also needs its words in what the stage-1 call said (two separate readings). Competing subjects (the "What caught your eye?" choices) pass G-SUBJECT, and Eat dish ideas use only references G-CATEGORY placed in Eat.
- **Additions.** Up to 4 extra candidates are appended to the reading. Only the slots left under the 5-candidate Qloo resolution cap are resolved (same rules, ≤ 3 concurrent); the results become new non-anchor references. A Qloo category with fewer than 3 cards is then filled from the new references only, after its published cards (`composeCategory` append mode: no delete, ranks continue). Research and dish ideas see the new references.
- **v2 media.** The media worker runs stage 1 per photo/frame (5 candidates) and marks the result `reading: "quick"` when every frame came from the qualified quick read. The display image it publishes for the capture is the best frame (the one most aggregated candidates cite), so the compile job's single stage-2 call reads that frame. Publishing never waits for stage 2.

### Style reading and "More like this"

Design: [`prp/2026-10-23-style-analysis/design.md`](../prp/2026-10-23-style-analysis/design.md) (sections A to E, amendments R-1 to R-20). Code: `packages/providers/src/vlm/style.ts` (schema, prompt, guard), `concentrate/style-provider.ts`, `packages/domain/src/style/*` (palette, vocabulary, wording, tag mapping, affinity matching, exemplars, reasons), `compiler/style-stage.ts` and `compiler/style-rails.ts`.

- **When it runs.** `StyleStage.plan` at job start: a usable `vision_style` reader (qualified, or experimental under `ANALYSIS_EXPERIMENTAL_ROLES`), no `experimental_analysis_enabled` kill for an experimental role (else the section is `held`, reason `paused`), the ledger able to reserve the call, and one vision call in the job budget (5 per analysis). Example replays get `empty`. The stage starts before observe and runs in parallel with it. For v2 photos the media worker already started the call next to stage 1 (`media_analysis_requests.style_state` / `style_observation`); the compile job waits for that reading instead of calling again.
- **Measure, read, guard.** Up to six swatches are measured on a 96x96 downsample (deterministic k-means in Lab, `style/palette.ts`; six and 96 px so a stripe or trim colour keeps its own swatch); the model only names swatches by index. The call (`atlas_style_reading_v1`, `vlm-style-v2`, 1024 px, 1100 output tokens, 18 s) receives the swatches and the closed tag vocabulary only, never stage-1 candidates. The wire schema is flat (no unions or nullable objects): the provider refused the first, nested schema ("compiled grammar is too large"). `guardStyleReading` (`guard-v18`) waits up to 3 s for the stage-1 guard (brand needs text stage 1 read, or the same brand published by stage 1, e.g. a logo it identified; `read_text` is downgraded without it; an unusable photo discards the reading) and applies G-STYLE-PERSON (including compound person nouns and possessives), G-STYLE-PERSON-TYPE with `GARMENT_COMPOUND_ALLOW`, G-STYLE-MOOD, G-STYLE-CULTURE (never asserted on a photo of people; held without two visible garment features), G-STYLE-OF, G-TEXT-ECHO, G-GENERIC (aesthetics only) and G-MEDIUM. A `tag_query` outside the closed list becomes null.
- **Wording (`sw-1`).** The server words every claim (`style/wording.ts`): aesthetics are asserted only at certainty >= 0.85 with a visible basis and two defining features (0.80 with same-family stage corroboration, 0.75 with a Qloo-entity one); era, lineage and mood are never asserted; materials at 0.70, construction and details at 0.65. G-STYLE-PALETTE keeps a palette name only when its swatch is present in the subject region (>= 8% of the region's pixels nearest to it) and is not the background (BG-1: the region leaves >= 15% of the image outside, the colour covers >= 25% of that outside part and is >= 1.6x more common outside than inside: a court around an outfit, a wall around a vitrine). The prompt (`vlm-style-v2`) also asks for the subject's own surface colours only, stripes and trims included. The stored form (`analyses.style_reading`, A.7) keeps certainty internally; the API sends display strings and a coarse `strength` only.
- **Qloo mapping (`smap-1`).** Exact acceptance only against `fixtures/qloo/style-vocab/vocab.json` (every id from a recorded real Qloo response; `scripts/style/harvest-vocab.ts` and `build-vocab.ts`) and, for interior, place, food and media subjects, at most 4 cached `/v2/tags` lookups per job. Asserted aesthetics (or ones a Qloo entity corroborates) become `filter.tags`; resembles claims are 0.5 signals; era and lineage 0.3. `target_*` and `market_archetype` tags never enter.
- **Garment rails (`smatch-1`, `style/affinity.ts`, `style/affinity-rail.ts`).** Exact tag mapping alone was too coarse for fashion: Qloo's style tags are long-tail ("Nautical Heritage", "Tennis Culture", signature pieces such as "Penny Loafer"), so specific readings mapped to nothing or to a broad tag half the catalogue carries ("Casual Wear", "Sportswear"), and the rails drifted to popular co-liked brands of other products, tiers and regions. Now the reading's own words (label, aesthetics and their visible features, lineage, era, materials, construction, details) are matched word by word against each brand's own Qloo tag labels, weighted by rarity across the 1000 harvested brands ((idf - 1)^1.5 / 2, capped at 3; colour words count little; a tag label the reading covers only partly counts less). Gates are product-level: the product comes from the label's head noun (garment, jeans, tailoring, dress, footwear, bag, jewellery, eyewear, watch, swim, lingerie) and Qloo's own `genre:brand:fashion:*` family tags decide what a brand makes (a garment rail keeps clothing labels, a shoe rail footwear labels); a casual reading drops couture, bridal and `$$$$` houses; a children's label stays off adult rails; a brand tied to a regional market the reading does not mention ("Brazilian Fashion", ethnic wear) is ranked down (x0.6); a gown house ranks far down for a suit. Two calls run in parallel: a taste call signalled by the identified brand (logo) and the 3 best-matching catalogue brands, filtered to the product's Qloo genre (footwear, bags, formalwear, jeans, ...), take 30; and a pool call that asks Qloo to rank the 30 best-matching catalogue brands (`filter.results.entities`), so they can be cards with a real response. Every result is gated and re-ranked by its own returned tags (80 % style, 20 % Qloo order); a card must share a style claim (not only the product noun), reach 35 % of the mean of the 3 best matches and an absolute floor; a shoe or bag rail with fewer than 4 matches is topped up from the genre-filtered taste list, saying which matched brand it shares a taste with. Reasons name the brand's own tag next to the reading's claim ("Known for nautical heritage, like the nautical Breton look in your photo"), never the palette. A brand anchor's early entity call is kept for this rail (gated with it) instead of being published unfiltered. A logo anchor joins a subject when its box lies mostly inside the subject (a chest print's IoU with a tee is tiny).
- **Other rails.** One insights call per subject (at most 2): places and food use the decor or restaurant-genre tag with the user's own city (`PUT /v1/me/city`, `filter.location.query`); media uses the work as entity signal or a style tag. An identified brand or work gets its entity call right after resolve (R-4). Hygiene: the flat-affinity detector (a flat list with a filter is kept at RRF weight 0), the city check, anchors removed, one brand listed twice collapsed, and, without an identified entity, only results that share an accepted tag (for garments: the primary style tag; too few results trigger a second call filtered by it). Lists are fused with weighted RRF (primary 1, secondary 0.6, overlap re-rank), capped at 3 per price level and 8 per subject. Each card's reason and `style_match` come from the real tag overlap (`style/reasons.ts`, `sr-1`). Rows are `recommendations` with `rail = 'more_like_this'`, `subject_id` and `reading_rev`.
- **Cross-domain.** `recommend()` runs once (R-5): after resolve and when the rail's primary returned, 4 s passed, or style ended. With no anchor at all it waits up to 15 s for the rail's bridges (the 2 most on-style rail results), since they are then its only signal. Bridge-driven cards say "Shares a taste with {bridge}, from your {aesthetic} rail". With a style reading, each category asks for 20 results and re-ranks them half by Qloo's order and half by how well each result's own tags match the reading. TOPIC-1: a room, building or dish with nothing else to signal (no entity, no city) gets a Watch rail of films Qloo tags with its topic (`urn:tag:theme:qloo:design`, `:architecture`, `:food`, popularity >= 0.5; recorded in `fixtures/qloo/probe-2026-10-24-tune`), reason "A film about {topic}, for the {label} in your photo". A resolved style tag becomes a Wear "style direction" card only when it is a fashion tag and Qloo returned no brand at all (a decor or architecture tag is not something to wear).
- **Title (TITLE-1).** When the stage-1 guard replaced the headline by "People in your photo", a fashion or media subject's guarded, person-free label becomes the title (re-applied at the end, after stage 2).
- **After stage 2.** `StyleStage.corroborate` raises wording (resembles to asserted), releases held claims and marks a vetoed claim `retracted` (listed in `style_reading.retracted`). Cards whose only overlap was the retracted claim are soft-hidden (`hidden_at`); others get their reason recomputed. Rails are never re-queried.
- **API.** `GET /v1/captures/{id}` adds `style_reading`, `rails[]`, card `reason` / `style_match` and `analysis.experimental`; `recommendations` stays the cross-domain list (<= 12). `GET /v2/media/{id}/analysis/{analysisId}` adds `experimental` and `result.style`. `POST /v1/captures/{id}/style/retry` re-runs a failed reading once (`style_retry` job: <= 6 Qloo requests, 1 vision call). Wire fixtures: `fixtures/native-contract/messages.json` (`capture_style_*`).

### Production experimental mode

`ANALYSIS_EXPERIMENTAL_ROLES` (Compose default `vision_photo,vision_photo_enrich,vision_video_frame,vision_style`) lets catalog-compatible, unqualified roles dispatch in any `NODE_ENV`, always marked experimental (`readiness().access = experimental_production`). They are charged to their own daily bucket (`ANALYSIS_EXPERIMENTAL_DAILY_SPEND_LIMIT_USD`) and a per-account cap (`ANALYSIS_EXPERIMENTAL_ACCOUNT_DAILY`); past either, a v2 photo waits with `waiting_reason: daily_limit` until the next UTC day. The kill switch is the feature flag `experimental_analysis_enabled` (`pnpm ops:kill`): the style section becomes `held` and v2 requests wait with `ai_paused`. `ANALYSIS_EXPERIMENTAL_ENABLED=false` removes the runners at startup (readiness `paused`). The demo override `CONCENTRATE_DEMO_ALLOW_ROLES` stays refused in production. Every client shows "Experimental" from `/v2/capabilities` (`availability.analysis.experimental`, `label`, `note`) and `analysis.experimental`.

### Example replays

`packages/domain/src/feed/examples.ts` holds a hand-reviewed reading for each "Try an example" photo (`outfit`, `room`, `cafe`). It is used only when an example capture is analyzed while no vision model can serve the job: the VLM is unavailable, or the job has no account and the only model is the account-budgeted Concentrate gateway (which refuses session-only jobs). Uploaded photos are never replayed. The observe stage records a `vlm` provider query with `is_replay = true` and model `reviewed-example`, so the inspector shows it as a replay. Resolution, Qloo categories and research then run as usual, so without a Qloo key Wear/Play/Watch still report `unavailable` and the "Related discoveries are taking longer." warning stays.

After that, the example's reviewed picks (public sample items from `feed/samples.ts`) fill categories that have no live results. They are stored with `origin = 'sample'` and no provider query ids, cite the example references they were chosen for as a creative rationale, and their sections become `partial`. Cards and the capture are labeled "Example result". Live results always take precedence.

### Budgets and resilience

- `JobBudget` (`compiler/budget.ts`): photo-reading model calls per job (up to 5: quick read, enrichment, two verifier checks when on, and the style reading), Qloo requests per job (default 18 since the style rails, design C.7; every HTTP attempt counts, including retries, across job attempts via `provider_queries.query_context.attempts`), and two retries per provider stage. The compiler prepays one request per Qloo call, so the limit holds even for adapters that ignore the budget. Lower-priority categories are `skipped` once it is spent.
- Adapters retry transient errors with exponential backoff and jitter, honor Retry-After, and trip a circuit breaker. A Retry-After longer than 15 s propagates instead, and the worker re-queues the job with that delay. An open circuit is transient too: its error carries the time until the breaker's next trial, and the worker re-queues with that delay.
- Qloo requests share one in-flight limit per process and key (`QLOO_MAX_CONCURRENCY`, default 3) across every caller, start at least `QLOO_MIN_SPACING_MS` (120 ms) apart in one process, and pass a cross-process token bucket in Postgres (`qloo_rate_buckets`, `QLOO_RATE_PER_SECOND` 8) with a daily limit (`QLOO_DAILY_REQUEST_LIMIT` 1,500). A 429 backs off at least 1100 ms.
- Soft deadline at 75% of `ANALYSIS_DEADLINE_MS` (45 of 60 s): research and dish ideas are skipped and what exists is published. The worker enforces the hard 60 s deadline per attempt.
- Non-retryable failures (or the last attempt) mark the capture `failed`, or `partial` if an analysis was already visible. A failure observed after cancellation is treated as cancellation.

### Evidence and the inspector

Every provider call writes a `provider_queries` row: sanitized request (no keys, no image bytes, no prompts), compact results (ids, names, ranks, per-query affinity and explainability for inspection only), status, timings and `is_replay`. Stage traces go to `trace_events`. Consumer DTOs never expose scores or provider names. Recommendations from replayed responses get `origin = 'replay'`, and their cards are `is_example`.

## Corrections (`services/analyses.ts`)

`submitCorrection` checks `expected_analysis_version` (409 when stale) and validates the referenced ids against the active analysis. It records a private `correct` preference event (global reference facts are never changed), bumps the taste revision, deletes active recommendations that relied on rejected references, cancels pending versions, and queues version N+1 with the correction in the payload.

## Deletion and purge

`deleteCapture` runs in one transaction under the capture lock. It cancels jobs and releases their dedupe keys, deletes dependent plans, the capture item (with its saves and memberships), preference links, analyses (cascading to private reference links, provider queries and recommendations), and items that only this capture's recommendations supported. It then marks the capture deleted with `purge_after = now() + 24h`. Media is deleted right after commit, and signed links stop working because the object is gone. The worker enqueues `purge_deleted` hourly (dedupe key per hour). It physically removes deleted captures, expired anonymous sessions with everything they own, expired uploads and their media, expired idempotency keys and feed snapshots.

## Music Mode (`packages/domain/src/audio/analyze-job.ts`)

- The recording is read from the capture-v2 namespace that `POST /v2/media/audio`
  wrote (the worker registers the shared multipart storage). A missing object
  fails the analysis with `storage_unavailable`; it is never reported as a
  provider outage.
- Fingerprinting (when a recognizer is configured) and the ElevenLabs Scribe
  transcript each propose artist names. Transcript queries come from "by X",
  DJ cues ("listening to X", "this is X") and capitalized runs, never from an
  arbitrary sentence. An artist resolves only when its full normalized name was
  heard; same-named hits go to the most popular, two different heard names
  stay ambiguous. Each signal keeps its own reason in the result.
- A resolved artist becomes a library item (Qloo id, https Qloo image) and
  starts one related discovery (`discovery_jobs.source = 'music_mode'`): Qloo
  insights from the artist across Watch, Play, Wear and Eat. Its cards join the
  interest feed with "Because you heard <artist>." and the analysis result
  (`GET /v2/media/{id}/analysis/{analysisId}`) returns `artist` plus live
  `related` cards and `related_state`.
- `GET /v2/media/{id}/tracks`, `/evidence` and `/transcript` read the stored
  track, evidence and transcript (transcripts are owner-only).
- Live check (2026-10-23, a 6 s spoken WAV): transcribed and identified
  Fleetwood Mac in about 1 s; related discovery finished in 7.5 s with Stevie
  Nicks, Electric Light Orchestra, Lost in Translation, Levi Strauss & Co.,
  Timberland and Strawberry Fields, all with Qloo images.

## Feed growth

- Pull-to-refresh starts an interest-expand wave each minute (categories
  rotate); plain polls start one at most every 15 minutes. A refresh moves the
  previous first page down so the top changes, and never drops a card.
- The pool takes capture recommendations, the taste seed, Music Mode
  suggestions and the owner's own taste-based "Discover more" results (Qloo
  only). `GET /v1/discoveries` reports `expansion {state, poll_after_ms}` while
  new cards are being fetched.

## Known gaps

- Targeted retry of individual sections (`payload.sections`) is accepted but not yet used: retries rerun the job, reusing completed stages.
- No real-provider smoke check has run here: there is no Qloo key, and Anthropic and Wikipedia are unreachable from the build sandbox.
